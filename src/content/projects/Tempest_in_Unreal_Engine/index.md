## Summary

Tempest is an Unreal Engine 5 project -- featuring a water-powered hero character and fire / combat-based enemy characters -- building an elemental clash theme of gameplay. Implemented as a single-player, wave-based survival experience, Tempest employs the Gameplay Ability System (GAS) for modular abilities and data-driven cooldowns / damage blocks, and backed by Niagara GPU particle systems for stylized, interesting effects and cues. The gameplay is orchestrated by a C++ finite-state wave manager actor and spanned by core mechanisms of spawn, battle waves, unlock new abilities, replenish mana, and repeat. Intelligent enemy behavior is implemented with Behavior Trees and Blackboard-driven AI.

The game was developed in approximately a month with a fellow Computer Graphics student at UPenn, [Daniel Gerhardt](https://github.com/danieljgerhardt).
